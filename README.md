
# Abdominal Obesity and Fall Risk Among Older Adults

**BIOSTAT 681 – Introduction to Causal Inference**  
University of Michigan | Fall 2026

## Project Overview

This project investigates the causal effect of abdominal obesity on subsequent fall risk among older adults using longitudinal data from the Health and Retirement Study (HRS).

The study applies causal inference principles and statistical methods to examine the effect of abdominal obesity on fall risk while accounting for potential confounding factors.

## Data Source

**Health and Retirement Study (HRS)**

The HRS is a longitudinal study of older adults in the United States, providing information on health conditions, physical functioning, demographic characteristics, and other relevant factors.

## Research Objectives

- Define a causal research question and target estimand.
- Identify potential confounders using a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG).
- Evaluate causal identification assumptions.
- Apply appropriate causal inference methods using R.
- Estimate and interpret causal effects with uncertainty assessment.

## Methodology

The analysis is guided by the potential outcomes framework and the following causal identification assumptions:

- **Consistency**
- **Conditional Exchangeability**
- **Positivity**

The final estimation strategy will be determined based on the research question, data structure, and identification assumptions.

## Tools and Technologies

- Causal Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs)
- Statistical Analysis and Data Visualization
